### **6.3 Arithmetic Progressions (AP)**

1. **Definition**: An arithmetic progression is a sequence of numbers in which the difference between consecutive terms is constant, known as the *common difference* (denoted by $d$).

**定义**：等差数列是一个数字序列，其中相邻两项之间的差是恒定的，称为*公差*（用 $d$ 表示）。

2. **General Formula**:

* The nth term of an arithmetic progression is given by:

$$

T_n = a + (n-1)d

$$

where $a$ is the first term and $d$ is the common difference.

**通用公式**：

* 等差数列的第 $n$ 项为：

$$

T_n = a + (n-1)d

$$

其中 $a$ 是首项，$d$ 是公差。

3. **Sum of First $n$ Terms**:

* The sum of the first $n$ terms of an arithmetic progression is:

$$

S_n = \frac{n}{2} [2a + (n-1)d]

$$

or equivalently,

$$

S_n = \frac{n}{2} (a + l)

$$

where $l$ is the last term.

**前 $n$ 项的和**：

* 等差数列前 $n$ 项的和为：

$$

S_n = \frac{n}{2} [2a + (n-1)d]

$$

或者等价地，

$$

S_n = \frac{n}{2} (a + l)

$$

其中 $l$ 是末项。

4. **Key Examples**:

* Example: Find the sum of the series: $3, 6, 9, 12, 15$.

* First term $a = 3$, common difference $d = 3$, number of terms $n = 5$.

* Using the sum formula:

$$

S_5 = \frac{5}{2} [2(3) + (5-1)(3)] = 5 \times 15 = 75

$$

**关键例子**：

* 例子：求和数列：$3, 6, 9, 12, 15$。

* 首项 $a = 3$，公差 $d = 3$，项数 $n = 5$。

* 使用求和公式：

$$

S_5 = \frac{5}{2} [2(3) + (5-1)(3)] = 5 \times 15 = 75

$$

### **6.4 Geometric Progressions (GP)**

1. **Definition**: A geometric progression is a sequence where each term is found by multiplying the previous term by a fixed, non-zero number called the *common ratio* (denoted by $r$).

**定义**：等比数列是一个序列，其中每项是通过将前一项乘以一个固定的非零数字（称为*公比*，用 $r$ 表示）来得到的。

2. **General Formula**:

* The nth term of a geometric progression is:

$$

T_n = a \cdot r^{n-1}

$$

where $a$ is the first term and $r$ is the common ratio.

**通用公式**：

* 等比数列的第 $n$ 项为：

$$

T_n = a \cdot r^{n-1}

$$

其中 $a$ 是首项，$r$ 是公比。

3. **Sum of First $n$ Terms**:

* The sum of the first $n$ terms of a geometric progression is:

$$

S_n = a \frac{1 - r^n}{1 - r} \quad \text{(for \( r \neq 1 \))}

$$

**前 $n$ 项的和**：

* 等比数列前 $n$ 项的和为：

$$

S_n = a \frac{1 - r^n}{1 - r} \quad \text{(当 \( r \neq 1 \))}

$$

4. **Sum to Infinity**:

* If the common ratio $|r| < 1$, the sum to infinity is:

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{a}{1 - r}

$$

where $a$ is the first term and $r$ is the common ratio.

**无穷和**：

* 如果公比 $|r| < 1$，无穷和为：

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{a}{1 - r}

$$

其中 $a$ 是首项，$r$ 是公比。

5. **Key Examples**:

* Example: Find the sum to infinity of the series: $2, 1, 0.5, 0.25, \dots$.

* First term $a = 2$, common ratio $r = \frac{1}{2}$.

* Using the sum to infinity formula:

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{2}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = 4

$$

**关键例子**：

* 例子：求无穷和数列：$2, 1, 0.5, 0.25, \dots$。

* 首项 $a = 2$，公比 $r = \frac{1}{2}$。

* 使用无穷和公式：

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{2}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = 4

$$

### **6.5 Infinite Geometric Series**

1. **Definition**: An infinite geometric series is a series with an infinite number of terms, where the terms follow the geometric progression pattern.

**定义**：无穷等比级数是一个无限项的级数，其中项遵循等比数列的模式。

2. **Convergence**:

* The series converges if the absolute value of the common ratio $|r| < 1$, and the sum to infinity is:

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{a}{1 - r}

$$

* If $|r| \geq 1$, the series diverges (does not sum to a finite value).

**收敛性**：

* 如果公比的绝对值 $|r| < 1$，级数收敛且无穷和为：

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{a}{1 - r}

$$

* 如果 $|r| \geq 1$，级数发散（不收敛到有限值）。

3. **Key Examples**:

* Example: For the series $2 + 1 + 0.5 + 0.25 + \dots$, the first term $a = 2$ and the common ratio $r = \frac{1}{2}$. The sum to infinity is:

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{2}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = 4

$$

**关键例子**：

* 例子：对于数列 $2 + 1 + 0.5 + 0.25 + \dots$，首项 $a = 2$，公比 $r = \frac{1}{2}$。无穷和为：

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{2}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = 4

$$
