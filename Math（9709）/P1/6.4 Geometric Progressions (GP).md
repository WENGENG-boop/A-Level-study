1. **Definition**: A geometric progression is a sequence where each term is found by multiplying the previous term by a fixed, non-zero number called the *common ratio* (denoted by $r$).

**定义**：等比数列是一个序列，其中每项是通过将前一项乘以一个固定的非零数字（称为*公比*，用 $r$ 表示）来得到的。

2. **General Formula**:

* The nth term of a geometric progression is:

$$

T_n = a \cdot r^{n-1}

$$

where $a$ is the first term and $r$ is the common ratio.

**通用公式**：

* 等比数列的第 $n$ 项为：

$$

T_n = a \cdot r^{n-1}

$$

其中 $a$ 是首项，$r$ 是公比。

3. **Sum of First $n$ Terms**:

* The sum of the first $n$ terms of a geometric progression is:

$$

S_n = a \frac{1 - r^n}{1 - r} \quad \text{(for \( r \neq 1 \))}

$$

**前 $n$ 项的和**：

* 等比数列前 $n$ 项的和为：

$$

S_n = a \frac{1 - r^n}{1 - r} \quad \text{(当 \( r \neq 1 \))}

$$

4. **Sum to Infinity**:

* If the common ratio $|r| < 1$, the sum to infinity is:

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{a}{1 - r}

$$

where $a$ is the first term and $r$ is the common ratio.

**无穷和**：

* 如果公比 $|r| < 1$，无穷和为：

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{a}{1 - r}

$$

其中 $a$ 是首项，$r$ 是公比。

5. **Key Examples**:

* Example: Find the sum to infinity of the series: $2, 1, 0.5, 0.25, \dots$.

* First term $a = 2$, common ratio $r = \frac{1}{2}$.

* Using the sum to infinity formula:

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{2}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = 4

$$

**关键例子**：

* 例子：求无穷和数列：$2, 1, 0.5, 0.25, \dots$。

* 首项 $a = 2$，公比 $r = \frac{1}{2}$。

* 使用无穷和公式：

$$

S_{\infty} = \frac{2}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = 4

$$
