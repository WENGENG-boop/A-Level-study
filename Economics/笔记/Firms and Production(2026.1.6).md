
### 1. Production & Productivity (生产与生产率)

*   **Labour-intensive:** Production processes that rely more on **human labour** than capital equipment. (Common in service sectors like hair salons).
*   **Capital-intensive:** Production processes that rely more on **machinery and technology** than labour. (Common in mass production like car manufacturing).
*   **Productivity:** The **efficiency** of factors of production. 
    *   Formula: $Productivity = \frac{Total Output}{Total Input}$
*   **Specialisation(专业化):** When a firm or worker concentrates on a **specific task**, leading to higher productivity but also **repetitive boredom**.

### 2. Costs of Production (生产成本)

*   **Fixed Cost (FC):** Costs that **do not change** with the level of output (e.g., Rent, Interest on loans).
*   **Variable Cost (VC):** Costs that **vary directly** with output (e.g., Raw materials, piece-rate wages).
*   **Total Cost (TC):** $TC = FC + VC$
*   **Average Cost (AC):** **Cost per unit** of output ($AC = \frac{TC}{Q}$). This is the most important indicator for **Competitiveness**.
*   **Average Fixed Cost (AFC):** This **constantly falls(持续下降)** as output increases because the fixed cost is spread over more units.

### 3. Economies of Scale (EOS - 规模经济)

*   **Internal Economies of Scale:** Long-run **Average Cost (AC) falls** as the **individual firm** increases its scale of production.
    *   **Purchasing Economies:** Bulk-buying leads to discounts.
    *   **Technical Economies:** Ability to afford large-scale, efficient machinery.
    *   **Financial Economies:** Large firms can borrow money at **lower interest rates**.
    *   **Managerial Economies:** Hiring specialist managers to improve efficiency.
*   **Internal Diseconomies of Scale:** Long-run **Average Cost (AC) rises** because the firm becomes too large.
    *   **Poor Communication:** Information flows too slowly in a large hierarchy.
    *   **Lack of Coordination:** Difficult to manage and monitor many departments.
    *   **Low Morale:** Workers feel alienated (like a "cog in the machine").

*   **External Factors:** Factors affecting the **entire industry**, causing the **AC curve to shift**.
    *   **External Economies:** A pool of **skilled labour** or better **infrastructure** in the area.
    *   **External Diseconomies:** **Congestion** (traffic jams) or **increased competition for resources** (driving up local rents).

### 4. Market Structures (市场结构)

*   **Perfect Competition:** 
    *   **Characteristics:** Many buyers/sellers, **Homogeneous products** (identical), **No barriers to entry/exit**.
    *   **Price Taker:** The firm must accept the market price.
*   **Monopoly:**
    *   **Characteristics:** **Single seller**, **Unique product**, **High barriers to entry** (Legal/Capital).
    *   **Price Maker:** The firm has significant **Market Power** to set prices.
    *   **Barriers to Entry:** Mechanisms that prevent new firms from entering (e.g., **Patents**, **Advertising/Branding**, **High Start-up Costs**).

### 1. 补充：Internal EOS (内部规模经济) 的另外两把“板斧”

*   **Marketing Economies (营销规模经济)**：
    *   **核心逻辑**：大公司即便花巨资打广告（如海报、电视广告），由于 **Output (产量)** 巨大，平摊到每一个产品上的广告费（Cost per unit）极低。
    *   **例子**：可口可乐在全球投1亿美金广告，但它每年卖出几十亿瓶，每瓶分担的广告费不到几美分。

*   **Risk-bearing Economies (风险承担规模经济)**：
    *   **核心逻辑**：大公司通常会 **Diversify (多元化)** 它们的产品线。如果一种产品卖得不好，还有其他产品在赚钱，从而分散了风险。
    *   **例子**：三星既卖手机，也卖冰箱、造船、做保险。手机业务受挫时，其他部门能撑住公司，这让大企业比小企业更稳健。

### 2. 补充：Market Structures (更加完整的市场类型)

除了两个极端，中间的这两个才是考试中最常考的“现实情况”：

*   **Oligopoly (寡头垄断)**：
    *   **Definition (定义)**：市场上只有 **Few large firms** (少数几家大公司) 占据了大部分市场份额。
    *   **Key Feature (关键特征)**：**Interdependence (相互依赖)**。也就是说，一个大公司改价格，其他公司必须跟着动。
    *   **Competitive behavior**：通常会发生 **Price Wars (价格战)**，或者为了避免两败俱伤而进行 **Non-price competition (非价格竞争)**（如比拼广告、赠品、售后）。
    *   **例子**：手机运营商（移动、联通、电信）、大型超市、民航飞机制造（波音和空客）。

*   **Monopolistic Competition (垄断竞争)**：
    *   **Definition (定义)**：有 **Many small firms** (很多小公司)，但它们的产品 **Differentiated (有差异化)**。
    *   **Key Feature**：因为产品有品牌区别，所以公司有一定的 **Price-making power (定价权)**，但不多。
    *   **No barriers to entry**：新店很容易开张。
    *   **例子**：奶茶店、理发店、服装店。每家奶茶味道不一样（差异化），但奶茶店极多（竞争激烈）。
