A person works at home making table cloths. He works for 6 hours each day and makes 15 table cloths an hour. He sells them for $10 each. He then decides to work for only 4 hours each day. What can be concluded from the above information?

C The daily opportunity cost would be 30 table cloths. / 每日的机会成本为 30 个桌布
